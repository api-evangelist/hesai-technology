Obstacle Detection Is Where the Conversation Begins When lidar is discussed in the railway industry, the conversation usually starts at the front of the vehicle. Operators, rolling stock manufacturers and system integrators are actively evaluating front-mounted lidar for trains and trams to improve obstacle detection and environmental awareness. It is a logical focus.
